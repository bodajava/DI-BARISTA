# Maison Folio Design System (E-Commerce Storefront)

> Couture black-and-ivory: a gigantic Bodoni Moda didone on warm ivory, one black ink, zero radius and a deliberate scale gap between hero and body. For fashion houses, lookbooks and atelier campaigns.

## Overview & Personality
- **Aesthetic**: Luxury Couture
- **Industry**: Commerce
- **Default Mode**: Light (`#f4f0e8` canvas, `#0e0d0c` ink)
- **Display Type**: Bodoni Moda (up to 168px at -0.05em tracking)
- **Body Type**: Inter Tight (15px)
- **Mono Type**: JetBrains Mono (12px)
- **Corners**: Zero radius (`0px`) everywhere
- **Container**: 1440px
- **Primary Action**: Solid black rectangle inverting to ivory outline on hover

## Tokens
- `--background`: `#f4f0e8` (light) / `#0b0a09` (dark)
- `--foreground`: `#0e0d0c` (light) / `#f4f0e8` (dark)
- `--card`: `#fbf8f2` (light) / `#141211` (dark)
- `--primary`: `#0e0d0c` (light) / `#f4f0e8` (dark)
- `--primary-foreground`: `#f4f0e8` (light) / `#0b0a09` (dark)
- `--border`: `rgba(14,13,12,0.2)` (light) / `rgba(244,240,232,0.2)` (dark)
- `--radius`: `0px`
