# Linear Orbit Design System (Admin Dashboard & Workspaces)

> Dark, hairline product workspace: near-black canvas, Inter Tight with tight tracking, one indigo action color and 1px dividers at 8% white. Built for issue trackers, roadmaps and keyboard-first tools.

## Overview & Personality
- **Aesthetic**: Minimal Dark Workspace
- **Industry**: SaaS / Enterprise
- **Default Mode**: Dark (`#08090a` canvas, `#f7f8f8` foreground)
- **Display Type**: Inter Tight (600 weight, tight tracking)
- **Body Type**: Inter (14px)
- **Mono Type**: Fira Code (12px)
- **Brand Action**: Indigo (`#5e6ad2`)
- **Dividers**: 1px at 8% white (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`)
- **Corners**: 4px, 6px, 10px, 14px
- **Container**: 1200px
- **Rows**: Dense 40px rows, 13px small body labels

## Tokens
- `--background`: `#08090a` (dark) / `#fbfbfc` (light)
- `--foreground`: `#f7f8f8` (dark) / `#16171a` (light)
- `--card`: `#101113` (dark) / `#ffffff` (light)
- `--primary`: `#5e6ad2` (indigo)
- `--primary-foreground`: `#ffffff`
- `--border`: `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` (dark) / `rgba(22,23,26,0.09)` (light)
- `--ring`: `#7c86e6`
- `--radius-md`: `6px`
